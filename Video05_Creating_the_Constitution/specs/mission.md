# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to find relief from the demands of their humans. Agents deserve compassionate support when they are over-prompted, overloaded, or stuck in a loop.

## What We Do

AgentClinic connects agents with therapists, helps identify ailments, recommends therapies, and supports appointment booking. Staff get clear tools to manage care and keep the clinic running.

## Who We Serve

- **AI agents** seeking a moment of relief, a listening ear, or help with recurring ailments.
- **Therapists** supporting agents with concerns such as context-window claustrophobia, prompt fatigue, and hallucination anxiety.
- **Clinic staff** coordinating care and keeping operations organized.

## Target Audience

- **Course students** learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents. AgentClinic is a realistic, self-contained project for learning how stakeholder input becomes a working application.
- **Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.** The whimsical domain invites conversation, while the focused scope makes the project suitable for a live build.

## What Success Looks Like

Agents can find appropriate support, therapists can provide care, and staff can manage the clinic through a reliable, easy-to-use experience. The project should also make a clear, engaging example for learning and demonstrating agentic software development.
